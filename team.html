Meet the Team

The students building Africa ICON.

Founder & Chair

Andrea Tchinda

Harvard College

Andrea Tchinda

Founder & Chair

A senior studying Statistics with a focus on data science, and a secondary in Sociology. She is passionate about using technology, especially data science, to answer some of Africa's core issues such as street network mapping. Beyond technology, she is passionate about policy: as a senior director at the Harvard Undergraduate Foreign Policy Initiative, she has written recommendations for USAFRICOM, USSOUTHCOM, and Anduril.

Co-Chair

Maxim Ndjikinya

Northeastern University

Maxim Ndjikinya

Co-Chair

A fourth year Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science student with leadership experience across NSBE, Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc., and events focused on innovation. His interests span technology, infrastructure, emerging markets, and African policy, with aspirations to advance sustainable infrastructure development in Cameroon.

Programming Lead

Kareemat Adeagbo

Boston University

Kareemat Adeagbo

Programming Lead

A sophomore studying Mechanical Engineering and Philosophy. As Director of International Projects for Engineers Without Borders, she leads multidisciplinary teams, including a clean water infrastructure initiative in Tinet, Kenya. She brings her experience in engineering and project leadership to shaping Africa ICON's programming.

Finance Lead

Priscilla Koranteng

Boston University

Priscilla Koranteng

Finance Lead

A sophomore studying Finance and African American & Black Diaspora Studies. Through leadership roles in BU's Black Business Student Association and Student Government, she has built experience in professional development, community engagement, and conference planning. She brings these experiences to Africa ICON with a passion for connecting students' professional pursuits to Africa's future.

Marketing Lead

Powj Nyibong

Arizona State University

Powj Nyibong

Marketing Lead

A junior studying Computer Information Systems and Economics at Arizona State University, Powj is passionate about the role of technology and education in advancing economic development in Africa. Through her work with the Mastercard Foundation and AI Acceleration at ASU, she contributed to AI initiatives supporting educational programs across Africa. As a leader within the African Students Association and NSBE, she brings a passion for storytelling and elevating the voices of young Africans to Africa ICON.

AFRICA ICON · The African Intercollegiate Conference

Harvard African Students Association
Harvard African Students Society
Harvard College

Africa ICON is a student-led and student-run conference, organized independently by the Harvard African Students Association, a Harvard College student organization, and the Harvard African Students Society, a University-wide organization sponsored by the Center for African Studies. As a student initiative, Africa ICON represents its organizers and does not represent Harvard University as a whole.
