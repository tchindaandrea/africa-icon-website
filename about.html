About

Who we are, our mission, and why Africa ICON exists.

What Is Africa ICON?

Africa ICON is the first intercollegiate African conference at Harvard University, bringing together ambitious and passionate undergraduates to explore Africa's intricacies, build meaningful connections, and step into the role they'll play in shaping the continent's present and future.

✊🏾

Our Mission

Putting young Africans at the center of the conversations and actions shaping Africa's present and future.

🌍

Pan-African Community

Connecting young Africans into one intercollegiate network.

🤝

Cross-Campus Exchange

Bringing together students from campuses across the country to share ideas, culture, and opportunity.

Africa ICON is a student-led and student-run conference, organized independently by the Harvard African Students Association, a Harvard College student organization, and the Harvard African Students Society, a University-wide organization sponsored by the Center for African Studies. As a student initiative, Africa ICON represents its organizers and does not represent Harvard University as a whole.
