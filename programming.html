Programming

How you'll engage at Africa ICON.

How You'll Engage

Two days, six ways to take part.

01

A-Series

Hear directly from people who've already done what you're working toward, sharing what they learned the hard way.

02

Technical Workshops

Practical sessions built around technical skills: the how behind building for Africa, not just the why.

03

Pitch Competition

Funding business ventures, nonprofits, and research projects. See the three tracks →
04

Keynote Addresses

African leaders and industry pioneers take the main stage to talk about what they've built and what they've learned along the way.

05

ICON Conversations

Intimate, highly conversational sessions where students lead the dialogue, engaging our guests directly and candidly.

06

Career Fair & Exhibition

A dedicated networking opportunity to connect with companies, organizations, and initiatives working across Africa.

Africa ICON is a student-led and student-run conference, organized independently by the Harvard African Students Association, a Harvard College student organization, and the Harvard African Students Society, a University-wide organization sponsored by the Center for African Studies. As a student initiative, Africa ICON represents its organizers and does not represent Harvard University as a whole.
